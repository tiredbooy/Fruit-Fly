# Project Design Context

## Project

- Name: FlyBrain Lab
- Product: scientific simulation observatory
- Stack: TypeScript, Three.js, Vite, Bun, aiohttp
- Mode: dark, compact, Persian RTL

## Source of truth

- Product constraints: `PRODUCT.md`
- Visual system: `DESIGN.md`
- Tokens and implementation: `frontend/web/src/styles.css`
- Permanent engineering rules: `AGENTS.md`

## Required patterns

- Dominant living specimen chamber with a restrained instrument rail.
- Cyan sensory/world signals, amber food/hunger, magenta motor/neural activity.
- Semantic controls, visible focus, reduced motion, and usable WebGL fallback.
- Exact official neuron IDs and labels; symbolic graphics must say they are not
  anatomical reconstructions.

## Avoid

- Frontend steering or behavior logic.
- Claims of thought, emotion, consciousness, or anatomical reconstruction.
- Remote fonts, analytics, decorative gradients, and generic dashboard chrome.
